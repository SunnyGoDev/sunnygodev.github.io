# Sunny Go Support — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sunny Go Support Discord bot (the "Bot"), operated by SunnyGoDev ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

The Bot only collects what it needs to run its features:

- **Discord IDs:** your user ID, and the IDs of servers, channels and roles where the Bot is used. These are numbers Discord assigns; they are needed to link settings and progress to the right user or server.
- **Command input:** the options you provide when you use a command (for example, a poll title or a setting value), as needed to carry out that command.
- **Feature data:** information created by using the Bot's features, such as server settings and in-bot game progress, currency or statistics.
- **Error logs:** technical details recorded when something goes wrong, which may include the command used and the relevant IDs.

The Bot does **not** read the content of your messages, and it does not collect your email address, password, IP address, or payment information.

## 2. How we use it

We use this information only to:

- Run the Bot's commands and features.
- Remember server settings and user progress.
- Find and fix bugs, and prevent abuse.

We do **not** sell, rent or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Storage and security

Data is stored in a database on a server operated by us. Access is limited to the Bot's developers. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage is completely secure.

## 4. Retention

We keep data for as long as it is needed for the Bot's features. You can request deletion at any time (see section 5). Error logs are kept only as long as needed to diagnose problems.

## 5. Your choices

- **Deletion:** you can ask us to delete the data associated with your Discord user ID. Server administrators can ask us to delete their server's data. Deleting data may permanently remove your in-bot progress.
- **Access:** you can ask what data the Bot holds about you.
- **Stop using the Bot:** you can stop using its commands at any time, and server administrators can remove the Bot from their server.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 8.

## 6. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone below the minimum age required by [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) in their country, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.
